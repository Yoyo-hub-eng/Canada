My Memories from Canada

August 6th - Arrival at the Airport and Suwan Academy

I arrived in Canada today! After a long flight from Shanghai, I was excited to explore this new country. I visited Suwan Academy and was impressed by the beautiful facilities and welcoming atmosphere. It felt wonderful to start my English learning journey in such an inspiring environment.

August 7th - A Shopping Adventure

Today I went grocery shopping in Canada for the first time! I discovered that things are more expensive than I expected, but I was delighted to find Master Kong Iced Black Tea. It reminded me of home while I explore this wonderful new place. The shopping experience gave me a chance to practice English and interact with locals in a real-world setting.

August 8th - Exploring the CN Tower and Ripley's Aquarium

What an unforgettable day! Here's how I spent my time:

  • 12:10 – 12:50 PM: I prepared my CityPASS and headed to the iconic CN Tower, Canada's most famous television and observation tower.
  • 12:50 – 3:00 PM: I took an amazing tour of the CN Tower. The panoramic views from the top were breathtaking! I could see the entire city of Toronto stretched out below me. The experience was both thrilling and a little exhausting, but absolutely worth it.
  • 3:30 – 5:00 PM: I used my CityPASS to visit Ripley's Aquarium of Canada, where I was amazed by the incredible marine life and underwater wonders. After exploring the aquarium, I returned to Suwan.

The highlight of the day was witnessing the beautiful sunset from the CN Tower—the sky was painted in shades of orange, pink, and purple. This experience reminded me why I love exploring new places and how fortunate I am to be in Canada.

August 9th - Communication and Friendship with Local Students

Today was special and memorable! I had the opportunity to communicate with local Toronto students and make new friends. From 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM, we gathered in the school's cafeteria to share our experiences and learn about each other's cultures. It was fascinating to exchange ideas and discover our common interests despite coming from different backgrounds. The interactions were genuine and warm, and I felt welcomed by the Canadian students. This experience not only improved my English communication skills but also gave me meaningful friendships that I will treasure.

August 10th - Discovering Aerospace Wonders

Today I explored the fascinating world of aerospace and technology! From 1:30 PM to 4:00 PM, I visited the Aerospace Center with my fellow students. We learned about aircraft design, space exploration, and cutting-edge engineering innovations. The interactive exhibits and knowledgeable guides helped me understand complex scientific concepts while improving my technical English vocabulary. This experience sparked my curiosity about careers in science and technology.

August 11th - Visiting the University of Toronto

Today I had the amazing opportunity to visit the University of Toronto! The campus is incredibly vast and diverse with so many different buildings and facilities. I have to say that the architecture is truly attractive and impressive. Walking through the historic halls and modern research centers, I felt inspired by the academic atmosphere and countless opportunities available to students. The university showcases Canada's commitment to quality education and innovation. This visit has given me much to think about regarding my future educational aspirations.

August 12th - McMaster University and the Magnificent Niagara Falls

This was one of the most thrilling and unforgettable days of my entire trip! It was packed with incredible experiences and breathtaking natural beauty.

  • 7:00 AM: We started early with a hearty breakfast to fuel us for the day ahead.
  • 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM: We departed for McMaster University and enjoyed approximately a one-hour guided tour. The campus was beautiful and innovative, showcasing Canada's commitment to quality education.
  • 10:00 – 11:00 AM: We left McMaster and began our journey to the legendary Niagara Falls. The anticipation built as we drew closer to this natural wonder.
  • 11:30 AM: We boarded the thrilling boat tour. The experience was exhilarating—the powerful rush of the waterfalls and the misty spray covering us created an unforgettable moment!
  • 12:30 – 1:30 PM: Our instructor led us on a scenic walk along the waterfall to its source. The breathtaking scenery—with cascading water, lush vegetation, and the sheer power of nature—left me speechless. It was truly one of the most stunning natural spectacles I have ever witnessed.
  • 1:30 – 3:00 PM: After our adventure, we enjoyed a well-deserved lunch break at Wendy's in the area, where we relaxed and shared our excitement about the incredible experiences.
  • 3:00 – 4:30 PM: We concluded our adventure with a leisurely stroll through the charming lakefront town, taking in the peaceful scenery and reflecting on this remarkable journey.

I cannot express enough how amazing this day was! The power and beauty of Niagara Falls exceeded all my expectations. Getting sprayed by the mist and feeling so close to such a magnificent natural wonder was truly an extraordinary experience. McMaster University was also impressive—the campus is beautiful and full of academic excellence. This day will remain forever in my heart!
